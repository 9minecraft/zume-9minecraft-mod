# Zume — Scout Distant Landscapes and Mobs with Smooth Zoom

Zume 9minecraft Mod gives you smooth camera zooming to inspect distant terrain, mobs, and structures. The view glides in when you hold the zoom key and returns smoothly once you let go. You can adjust the magnification on the fly using your mouse wheel or dedicated hotkeys. This mod works purely on the client side, meaning you can look across faraway landscapes or take third-person screenshots without server setup.

Smooth zoom animations glide in and out gently instead of snapping suddenly.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Nolij**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
